Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: April 26, 2026 | Effective Date: April 26, 2026
Your Privacy is Our Priority

My Daily Memory is designed with privacy at its core. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS application. Please read it carefully to understand our practices regarding your data.

1. Overview

My Daily Memory is a personal journaling application for iOS devices. The app allows you to:

  • Write daily journal entries
  • Track your moods and emotions
  • Organize entries with tags
  • Search and browse past memories
  • Set reminders for journaling
✓ No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, journal entries, or usage information to external servers. Everything stays on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

My Daily Memory does not collect, access, or transmit:

  • ❌ Journal entry content or text
  • ❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • ❌ Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Crash reports or diagnostics
  • ❌ Contacts or social media information
  • ❌ Financial or payment information

3. How Your Data is Stored

3.1 Local Storage

All journal entries, tags, moods, and app settings are stored exclusively on your iOS device using:

  • SwiftData / CoreData: Apple's native local database frameworks
  • iOS Secure Storage: Data is encrypted using iOS device encryption
  • App Sandbox: Data is isolated from other apps

3.2 iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable iCloud sync:

  • Your encrypted journal data is stored in your personal iCloud account
  • Data is synced across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data
  • Sync uses Apple's CloudKit framework with end-to-end encryption
  • You can disable iCloud sync at any time in the app settings or iOS Settings
Important: iCloud data is managed entirely by Apple, not by us. We never see or access your iCloud data.

4. Permissions We Request

Permission Purpose Required?
Notifications Send daily journaling reminders (if enabled by you) Optional
Face ID / Touch ID Secure app lock (if enabled by you) Optional
Photo Library Add photos to journal entries (future feature) Optional

All permissions are requested only when needed (just-in-time) and include clear explanations of why we need them. You can revoke permissions at any time through iOS Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

My Daily Memory does not integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • ❌ No analytics or tracking SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • ❌ No advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • ❌ No crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • ❌ No social media integrations
  • ❌ No cloud storage providers (except Apple iCloud, if you enable it)

The app uses only Apple's native iOS frameworks and does not communicate with any external servers.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your data with anyone because we do not collect or have access to it.

6.1 Export Feature

The app includes an export feature that allows you to export your journal entries to:

  • PDF documents
  • Markdown files
  • Plain text files

Exported files are saved to locations you choose (Files app, email, etc.). You have full control over where these files go and who can access them.

6.2 Legal Requirements

Since we do not collect or store your data on our servers, we have no data to provide to law enforcement or government agencies, even if legally required to do so.

7. Children's Privacy

My Daily Memory does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and does not contain any age-restricted content.

Parents can use iOS Screen Time and parental controls to manage their children's app usage.

8. Your Rights and Control

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

8.1 Access

You can access all your journal entries at any time through the app.

8.2 Edit or Update

You can edit or update any journal entry directly in the app.

8.3 Delete

You can delete individual entries, tags, or all data through the app settings. Uninstalling the app will also delete all local data.

8.4 Export (Data Portability)

You can export all your data in standard formats (PDF, Markdown, text) at any time.

8.5 iCloud Data Management

If you use iCloud sync, you can manage your iCloud data through:

  • App Settings → Disable iCloud Sync
  • iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage → My Daily Memory → Delete Data

9. Data Security

We implement several security measures to protect your data:

  • iOS Device Encryption: Data is encrypted using iOS native encryption
  • App Lock: Optional Face ID/Touch ID/passcode protection
  • App Sandbox: Data is isolated from other apps
  • No Network Transmission: Your journal content never leaves your device
  • No Server Storage: We don't operate any servers, so there's no server-side breach risk
Best Practice: Enable device passcode/biometrics and app lock for maximum security.
