Privacy Policy

Introduction

My Daily Memory ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your journal entries and personal information.

Core Principle: My Daily Memory is designed with privacy first. All journal entries and personal data are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, access, or transmit your journal content to any external servers.

Information We Access

My Daily Memory requires minimal permissions to function:

  • Local Storage: To save your journal entries on your device
  • iCloud (Optional): If you enable sync, encrypted data is stored in your personal iCloud account
  • Photos (Optional): If you choose to add photos to entries, we request access to your photo library

How Your Data is Stored

  • 100% Local Storage: All journal entries are stored exclusively on your device using iOS secure storage (SwiftData/CoreData)
  • No Cloud Upload: We do not upload your journal content to any third-party servers or our own servers
  • Optional iCloud Sync: If you enable iCloud sync, your encrypted journal data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. Only you can access this data
  • End-to-End Security: iCloud data is encrypted both in transit and at rest

What We Do NOT Collect

My Daily Memory does not collect, track, or access:

  • ❌ Your journal entries or personal writings
  • ❌ Analytics or usage data
  • ❌ Device information or identifiers
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Contacts or personal information
  • ❌ Crash reports or diagnostics (unless you explicitly submit feedback)

Third-Party Services

My Daily Memory does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app uses only Apple's native frameworks:

  • SwiftUI/UIKit: For the user interface
  • SwiftData/CoreData: For local data storage
  • CloudKit (Optional): For iCloud sync, managed entirely by Apple
  • PhotoKit (Optional): For photo library access

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone because we do not have access to it. Your journal entries never leave your device or personal iCloud account.

Export: You can export your journal entries at any time using the app's export feature (PDF, Markdown, or text). You control where these exports are saved.

Children's Privacy

My Daily Memory does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages. Parents can use iOS parental controls to manage their children's app usage.

Your Rights and Controls

  • Full Ownership: You own all your journal entries and data
  • Delete Anytime: Delete individual entries, tags, or all data through the app settings
  • Export Anytime: Export your complete journal in multiple formats
  • Disable iCloud: Turn off iCloud sync at any time in the app or iOS Settings
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app removes all local data. iCloud data can be deleted through iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage

Data Security

We implement several security measures:

  • Secure Storage: Data is encrypted using iOS native encryption
  • Device Security: Access protected by your device passcode/biometrics
  • No Remote Access: No server-side authentication or remote access to your data
  • Open Source Libraries: We only use well-vetted, open-source iOS frameworks

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in features or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
