Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I get started?

Simply download My Daily Memory from the App Store, open the app, and start your first entry. No sign-up or account required!

💾 Where is my data stored?

All your journal entries are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, access, or transmit any of your personal data to external servers.

Optional: You can enable iCloud sync to backup your data across your Apple devices. iCloud data is encrypted and only accessible by you.

🔐 Is my data secure?

Yes! My Daily Memory uses multiple security layers:

  • iOS device encryption for local storage
  • Optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock
  • No internet connection required (your data never leaves your device)
  • End-to-end encryption for iCloud sync (if enabled)

📱 Which devices are supported?

My Daily Memory requires iOS 17.0 or later and works on:

  • iPhone (all models with iOS 17+)
  • iPad (all models with iOS 17+)

💡 How do I track my activities?

From the home screen, you can:

  • Tap any category (Record, Cost, Diet, Exercise) to add entries
  • View your weekly summary at a glance
  • Plan tomorrow's activities using the "Plan Tomorrow" button
  • Use the search feature to find past entries

🏷️ Can I organize my entries?

Yes! You can create custom tags and categories to organize your journal entries. Filter entries by tag to quickly find related memories.

📤 Can I export my data?

Yes! My Daily Memory allows you to export your journal in multiple formats:

  • PDF documents (perfect for printing or sharing)
  • Markdown files (for use with other apps)
  • Plain text files

You have complete control over where these files are saved and who can access them.

🔔 How do reminders work?

You can set up daily reminders to help build a journaling habit:

  • Go to Settings → Reminders
  • Enable notifications
  • Choose your preferred time
  • Receive gentle reminders to journal

Reminders are optional and can be disabled at any time.

🌙 Does the app support Dark Mode?

Yes! My Daily Memory automatically adapts to your iOS system appearance settings. Switch between Light and Dark modes in iOS Settings → Display & Brightness.

🆓 Is the app free?

Yes! My Daily Memory is completely free to download and use. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or hidden fees.

🔄 How do I sync across devices?

Enable iCloud sync in Settings to automatically sync your journal across all your Apple devices (iPhone, iPad) signed in with the same Apple ID.

🗑️ How do I delete my data?

To delete all your data:

  • Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete All Data
  • Confirm your choice
  • Alternatively, uninstalling the app will also delete all local data

iCloud data: If you enabled iCloud sync, manage your data through iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage → My Daily Memory → Delete Data.

🐛 I found a bug. How do I report it?

We appreciate your feedback! Please email us with:

  • Your iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • A description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the bug (if applicable)

Send bug reports to: support@mydailymemory.app

💬 How can I request a new feature?

We'd love to hear your ideas! Email your feature requests to support@mydailymemory.app. We read every suggestion and consider them for future updates.
